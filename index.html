Cost breakdown

Leak rate

  1. Orifice discharge: Q = Cd A sqrt(2 P / rho), A = pi d^2/4, P = gauge pressure at the hole.
  2. Line actively flowing, no isolation: pressure held for the whole leak. V = Q (t_detect + t_stop). Q optionally capped at line throughput.
  3. CPM flag: Q vs the stated % of throughput. Below threshold, detection relies on rounds / patrol.
  4. Cd for corrosion pinholes is uncertain; L/d = wall/hole > 1 behaves more like a short tube (Cd ~0.8). Re < ~4000 lowers Cd.

Pad travel

  1. Oil runs downslope as a laminar gravity film of width W. Unit flow q = Q/W. Film depth h = (3 mu q / (rho g S))^(1/3), film velocity v = q/h, S = sin(atan(slope)).
  2. The front advances only as fast as it fills the film plus surface retention d_r, while infiltration f removes oil over the wetted area: W (h + d_r) dx/dt = Q - f W x.
  3. Solution: max run-out x_max = Q / (f W); time to reach x: t = -(h + d_r)/f ln(1 - x/x_max); with f = 0, t = x W (h + d_r) / Q.
  4. Distance is measured from where the jet lands. Jet throw, spray/mist and evaporation are not modelled. W is user-set; on a planar pad the stream may widen, which slows the front.
  5. Surface presets are screening values only; replace them with site data if available.

Cost

  1. Response = mobilization + unit cost x spilled bbl (free-product recovery, vac trucks, absorbents, crews).
  2. Unrecovered oil = spilled bbl x (1 - recovery %) x oil price.
  3. Impacted soil (yd3) = unrecovered oil volume / soil oil retention (porosity x residual oil saturation). Split on/off pad by wetted length at the time the leak is stopped: off-pad share = (x - pad edge) / x.
  4. Soil remediation = on-pad yd3 x on-pad $/yd3 + off-pad yd3 x off-pad $/yd3. Waste disposal = total yd3 x bulk density x $/ton.
  5. Lost revenue = lost production (bbl/d) x outage (h) / 24 x oil price. Repair is fixed per event. Both are independent of spill volume.
  6. Excludes regulatory penalties, third-party claims, groundwater remediation and long-term monitoring.
